What the numbers say

Option A fixes the label but fails a different rule. Near-black on bright orange is comfortably readable at 5.94:1, but the fill itself sits at 2.88:1 against the near-white canvas — under the 3:1 that WCAG 1.4.11 requires for a UI component's boundary. The button becomes hard to locate rather than hard to read. It also inverts the convention that a filled primary carries light text, so it reads as a highlight or a warning chip.

Option B clears every threshold — 4.90:1 label, 4.69:1 against the light canvas, 3.39:1 on the dark card, 6.06:1 on hover. It is the recommendation.

Both options keep --brand: hsl(24 95% 50%) untouched for non-text and large-text use — the active nav chip, stat accents, chart series and the sidebar highlight below. Only the interactive pair changes, so the brand's presence on screen is preserved.
